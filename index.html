Ph.D. in chemistry from MIT
[CV] [Google Scholar] [Twitter]

My research focuses on AI + Chemistry, with the goal of automating molecule generation and evaluation.

At MIT, I was advised by Prof. Yogesh Surendranath during my PhD and by Prof. Yuriy Roman during my postdoc. At NYU, I am advised by Prof. Kyunghyun Cho, and at Meta I am mentored by Dr. Ricky Chen and Dr. Ben Miller.

Current Research Areas

  • Generative modeling of 3D molecular conformations.
  • Large language models (LLMs) for chemistry prediction.
  • Environment-aware molecule design.

News

  • 2026: I received a Poster Award at the Gordon Research Conference on AI for Materials, Energy, and Chemical Sciences for EVA-Flow: A Unified Environment-Aware Generative Model for 3D Molecular Conformations.
  • August 8, 2025: Our Consistency paper is out in Digital Discovery! We evaluate how consistently LLMs answer when the same molecule is given as a SMILES string versus an IUPAC name, and find low consistency even after mapped training and KL regularization, suggesting that LLMs may capture surface-level patterns rather than underlying chemistry. {% include icon-twitter.svg %}
  • July 26, 2024: Our CatScore paper is out in Digital Discovery! We introduce CatScore, a learning-based metric for evaluating asymmetric catalysis in organic chemistry that enables efficient, effective assessment of diverse catalyst-design models at both the instance and system levels. {% include icon-twitter.svg %}
